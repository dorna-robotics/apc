# LCR Meter Calibration

The meter's open / short calibration, done at the start of a run. The run
moves the cathode and waits; you press the buttons on the meter.

Turn it on or off on the setup screen: **Measurement → Meter calibration**
(Off by default).
The calibration lasts until the meter is switched off, so after a power
cycle, run with Meter calibration **On**.

Before the run, set up the meter as in **LCR Meter Startup**.

## 1. Start the run
The run clears the anode, then sets the meter to C and D at 1 kHz and
hands its buttons back to you (**RMT** goes off).

## 2. Open (1 of 2)
The pendant shows **Meter calibration — open (1 of 2)**. The cathode is up
and the anode is clear.

On the meter, hold **REL** for 2 seconds, then press **REL** once. When the
normal reading is back, press **Resume** on the pendant.

## 3. Short (2 of 2)
The cathode comes down onto the bare anode. The pendant shows
**Meter calibration — short (2 of 2)**.

On the meter, hold **REL** for 2 seconds, then press **REL** once. When the
normal reading is back, press **Resume** on the pendant.

## 4. Done
The cathode goes up, the run takes the meter back (**RMT** on) and starts
on the first disc.

---

The run cannot see whether the meter took the calibration. If a step went
wrong, park and start a new run with Meter calibration **On** — it does
both steps again.

## Quick reference

| Step | Cathode | On the meter | Then |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open | Up, anode clear | Hold REL 2 s, press REL | Resume |
| Short | Down on the bare anode | Hold REL 2 s, press REL | Resume |
